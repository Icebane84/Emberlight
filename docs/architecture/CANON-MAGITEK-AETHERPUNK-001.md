## 1. The Setting & Player Role: The Expedition Commander

* **The World Above (The Silent Dusk):** The surface of the world has fallen to an age-long cosmic freeze where the sun is cold and atmospheric aether has crystallized into permafrost. The remnants of civilization survive inside **The Iron Redoubt** (The Bastion of the Hearth-Spire)—a colossal, steam-vented mountain fortress anchored to the magma veins of an ancestral volcano.
* **The World Below (The Umbral Catacombs):** Directly beneath the foundations of the Redoubt descends the infinite, non-Euclidean underworld known as the **Shifting Deep**. These catacombs are not static caves; they are a living, unstable geological organism permeated by chaotic leylines. Hallways dilate, collapse, and re-knit in response to ambient psychic resonance.
* **The Player's Role:** **You are the Expedition Commander.** Stationed within the reinforced core of the Redoubt's High Command Sanctum—behind heavy riveted bulkheads, hissing pneumatic copper conduits, and spinning flywheels—you direct the expeditionary vanguard remotely. You do not trudge through the mud; you command, diagnose, guide, and protect from the central dais.
